FIT LIFE goal physique model
================================

Mesh: MakeHuman base mesh (hm08) morphed to a young male, high muscle, lean weight.
Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (dedicated September 2020).
Copyright holders at release: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier.
Macro targets also include earlier work by Manuel Bastioni, released as CC0.

Skin texture: "toigo_light_skin_male_bronze" (young_lightskinned_male_diffuse_Bronze)
Author: Margaret Toigo
Pack: MakeHuman Community skins02 (skins02_cc0.zip)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html

The baked mesh is the CC0 young male retargeted to a lean athletic build (hero3d14): natural waist instead of a wasp pinch, lat and ribcage flare pulled in, defined but human abs/chest/shoulders, short hair. Still not a likeness.
The figure is a generic anatomical reference for the 87–90 kg / 12–15% goal.
It is not a likeness of Chi Wai. Athletic shorts are a clipped slice of the MakeHuman helper-tights shell
(same CC0 mesh) so the hero stays a physique study rather than a nude render.

Baked file: models/goal-physique.glb
